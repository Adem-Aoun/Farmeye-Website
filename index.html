Why FarmEye

Help every farmer see their fields clearly, find problems early, and decide with confidence.

FarmEye combines drones and AI to improve efficiency and productivity, with precise 2D maps, real-time detection, classification and counting.

FarmEye OS: one stack, from drone to decision.

FarmEye OS is the whole system. It flies the drone, finds what is in your fields, puts it on a map, and gives you one place to run it all: the FarmEye App.

  1. Drone and camera

    Flies your field and captures high-resolution imagery.

  2. AI detection

    Detects and classifies crops, weeds, trees and equipment.

  3. 2D mapping

    Pins every detection on a precise map and counts it.

  4. FarmEye App

    Plan the mission, watch the flight, review the map. This is where you work.

What we map.

Tell us what to look for. We tailor the scan and the labels to your farm.

Crop and tree mapping

Where your crops and trees are, how many there are, and how healthy they look, across the whole field.

Weed and pest detection

  1. Book a scan

  2. Choose what to map

  3. We fly your fields

    A drone with a high-resolution camera captures the whole area.

    Objects are detected, classified and pinned on a 2D map, ready for you to review.
